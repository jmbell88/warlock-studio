The question this corpus was retained for is answered: open-form subjects
the audit flags are usable meshes two times in five. The awning on posts and
the basin are real gaps the reviewer accepted; the bellows arm, the bow and
the clawed stand are gaps the reviewer did not, and none of the three was
tagged `holes` — they were wrong as forms, not perforated. So on v0.6.0 the
0.07 trigger measures topology, not defects, and
[`2026-09-02-hole-audit-vs-grade.md`](2026-09-02-hole-audit-vs-grade.md)
records that as the second half of the reroll's retirement. The one grade
that the audit could not have predicted is the elf mage at −3 on a 0.000
audit: a closed, well-formed mesh of the wrong figure, which is the
single-view humanoid problem and not this document's.
